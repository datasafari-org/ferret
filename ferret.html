Keywords — what should the page contain?

Plain terms are AND'd. Wrap phrases in quotes for exact match. Prefix with - to exclude. Group alternatives with OR.

Terms

Type, press Enter to add. Use the mode selector to change how the term is treated.

03

Placement — where in the page?

Restrict to the title, URL, visible body, or anchor text. Use the plural variant (allintitle: etc.) when every term must appear.

intitle: word must appear in the <title>

inurl: word must appear in the URL

intext: word must appear in the visible body

inanchor: word must appear in link anchor text

All-of variants

Every term in the box must appear. Google interprets these differently — they don't stack neatly with other operators, so use them alone or as the dominant filter.
